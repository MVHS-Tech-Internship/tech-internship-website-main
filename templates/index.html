Speaking at FETC 2026

The MVHS Tech Internship has been invited to speak at the Future of Education Technology Conference. Our team will deliver three sessions on student-led innovation, real-world AI implementation, and practical frameworks for supporting technology adoption in schools and districts.

FETC is one of the nation's largest and most influential ed-tech conferences, bringing together educators, industry leaders, and innovators from around the world. We're honored to represent student leadership on a national stage.
