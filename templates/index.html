Upcoming Events

FETC 2026

The MVHS Tech Internship has been invited to speak at the Future of Education Technology Conference (FETC) in January 2026. Our team will deliver three sessions, sharing insights on student-led innovation, real-world implementation of AI tools, and practical frameworks for supporting technology adoption in schools and districts.

FETC is one of the nation's largest and most influential ed-tech conferences, bringing together educators, industry leaders, and innovators from around the world. We're honored to represent student leadership on a national stage and contribute to shaping the future of educational technology.

How We Bring This to Life

Our work is guided by a set of core principles that shape how we build, collaborate, and create impact. These values help us make meaningful, student-led contributions both within our community and beyond.

  • Student leadership. Every project is led and executed by students who take ownership from idea to impact.
  • Innovation with purpose. We build tools, programs, and systems that solve real problems and meet real needs.
  • Accessibility. We make emerging technology understandable and usable for students, educators, and communities.
  • Collaboration. We work with partners and peers to expand our reach and create solutions that matter.
