Partnerships

We collaborate with innovative organizations to bring cutting-edge tools and resources to students, educators, and our broader community.

Featured Partner: Knowt

Knowt is an AI-powered study platform that helps students learn more effectively through intelligent note-taking, practice questions, and personalized study tools. Through our collaboration, students in our program receive free access to both Knowt’s standard features and its premium version, allowing them to explore the platform in real academic settings.

As part of this partnership, our interns use a student-designed evaluation rubric to assess the platform from a genuine learner’s perspective. They document their experiences, analyze how the tool aligns with real study needs, and share structured feedback directly with Knowt.

This collaboration gives Knowt meaningful insight into the student experience while giving our interns hands-on practice evaluating emerging AI tools in education.
