We collaborate with innovative organizations to bring cutting-edge tools and resources to MVHS students, enhancing learning experiences and expanding opportunities for growth.

Knowt Partnership

Knowt is an AI-powered study platform that helps students learn more effectively through intelligent note-taking, practice questions, and personalized study tools. Through our partnership, MVHS students have access to Knowt's platform, enabling them to create study materials, practice for exams, and enhance their learning with AI-assisted features.

This partnership supports our mission of integrating practical AI tools into education, giving students hands-on experience with technology that directly benefits their academic success.
