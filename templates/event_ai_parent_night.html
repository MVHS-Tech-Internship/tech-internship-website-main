On September 29, the MVHS Principal's Tech Internship hosted its second annual AI and Education Parent Night. Sixty-five parents came to explore one question: what does AI mean for our students?

Before the night, our interns surveyed 148 parents and 74 staff members. Most parents wanted to know where the line falls between appropriate and inappropriate use of AI, and more than half of teachers said they already use AI to create assignments and activities. Four guest speakers took up those findings, and then parents brought their own questions to the panel.
